Heart Disease Prediction System

Machine-learning web app

  • End-to-end pipeline that predicts cardiovascular risk from 11 clinical inputs, including age, chest pain type, cholesterol, resting ECG, maximum heart rate and ST slope.
  • One-hot encoding and StandardScaler for preparation, with inference features aligned to the training columns so predictions match the trained model.
  • Logistic Regression classifier deployed on Streamlit, returning High Risk or Low Risk with a probability percentage.
